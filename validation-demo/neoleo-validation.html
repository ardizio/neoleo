3 · How good are today's warnings?

2,167 real conjunction events from ESA (2015–2019). For each, we take the last CDM available 2 days before closest approach, apply NeoLEO's alert thresholds (Pc ≥ 1e-5), and compare with the final CDM.

Two days out, today's data misses about 1 in 3 high-risk events, and almost half of its alerts are false. That is the gap NeoLEO's fusion with operator data targets.

Data: NASA CARA Analysis Tools, PcTestCaseCDMs (NASA Open Source Agreement), github.com/nasa/CARA_Analysis_Tools · ESA Kelvins Collision Avoidance Challenge dataset, test set (CC BY 4.0), zenodo.org/records/4463683. Demo created for the SVST hackathon.
